About Us

Built by Researchers, For Researchers

SparkVy Advanced Research and Innovations was established by a team of experts in materials science, nanotechnology, synthesis, and analysis — serving applications from energy storage and sensors to biomedical and water treatment solutions.

Our mandate is to develop materials and processes of industrial importance. SparkVy houses a dedicated Centre of Excellence with high-end characterization and testing facilities, with a core focus on microstructure–structure–property correlations.

We also offer hands-on training and internships for students and faculty interested in supercapacitors, water splitting, batteries, solar cells, and anti-bacterial applications.

🎯 Mission

To advance science through pioneering research addressing real-world challenges, fostering innovation, interdisciplinary collaboration, and high-impact solutions.

🌐 Vision

To become a global catalyst for transformative discovery, fostering an interdisciplinary ecosystem where curiosity meets purpose and drives societal progress.
